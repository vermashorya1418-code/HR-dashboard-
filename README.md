# HR Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing employee attrition using SQL, Power BI, and DAX. The dashboard provides insights into employee turnover, identifies patterns associated with attrition, and highlights areas where HR can improve employee retention.

## Objectives
- Measure overall employee attrition and attrition rate.
- Identify key factors associated with employee turnover.
- Analyse attrition across departments, job roles, age groups, and overtime status.
- Understand employee exit reasons and satisfaction-related patterns.
- Support data-driven HR decisions and retention strategies.

## Tools & Technologies
- **SQL:** Data querying and analysis.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development.
- **DAX:** KPI calculations and analytical measures.
- **Power Query:** Data preparation and transformation.
- **CSV:** Data source format.

## Dataset
The project uses four tables:
- `Dim_Employee` – Employee demographics, job roles, overtime, and exit reasons.
- `Fact_Attrition` – Employee attrition status, income, satisfaction, and related metrics.
- `Dim_Department` – Department information.
- `Dim_Date` – Calendar and date-related information.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Employees
- Attrition Count
- Active Employees
- Attrition Rate
- Average Monthly Income
- Average Years at Company
- Average Weekly Working Hours
- Average Job Satisfaction

## Key Findings
- The dataset contains 1,500 employees, of whom 226 have attrited.
- The overall attrition rate is approximately **15.07%**.
- Employees working overtime exhibit a higher observed attrition rate than those not working overtime.
- Employees aged 26–35 show comparatively higher attrition.
- Work-life imbalance, poor manager relationships, and better compensation are among the major recorded exit reasons.

## Dashboard Features
- Interactive KPI cards for employee and attrition metrics.
- Department-wise and job-role-wise attrition analysis.
- Attrition comparisons by age group and overtime status.
- Analysis of employee exit reasons.
- Interactive slicers for filtering and exploring employee data.

## Business Insights & Recommendations
The findings suggest that HR should investigate workload distribution, work-life balance, managerial relationships, compensation competitiveness, and career development opportunities. These areas may help inform targeted employee-retention initiatives.

## Conclusion
The HR Attrition Dashboard converts employee data into actionable insights by measuring turnover and highlighting patterns associated with employee exits. It supports data-driven workforce planning and helps HR identify areas requiring further investigation.

**Note:** The dashboard presents descriptive patterns and associations. These findings do not independently establish the causes of employee attrition.
